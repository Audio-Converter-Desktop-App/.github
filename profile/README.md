## 🚀 Overview
Audio Converter for Windows is an audio file converter that changes the format of the recordings you already own — for example turning a WAV or FLAC file into MP3, or the other way around. Beyond format conversion, it works as a video to audio converter that pulls the sound track out of your own video files, and as an audio to text converter that runs speech-to-text transcription right on your desktop. Everything happens locally on your machine, so your files stay on your PC while you work with them.

## ✨ Features
- **Convert between audio formats** — Switch recordings from one format to another, including a fast audio converter to MP3 path for everyday playback.
- **Extract audio from video** — Use it as a video to audio converter to save the sound from a video file you already have as a standalone audio track.
- **Transcribe audio to text** — Built-in speech-to-text turns spoken recordings into editable text so you can search, quote, or repurpose what was said.
- **Batch processing** — Queue up several files and convert them together instead of one at a time.
- **Simple, focused interface** — Pick a file, choose a target format, and start — no clutter, no guesswork.
